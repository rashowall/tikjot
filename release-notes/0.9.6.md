A lighter tikjot while the timer runs, and a fix for the menu bar freezing with two monitors.

## Improved

- While you're away from your Mac (idle or locked), tikjot doesn't keep retrying an unreachable Jira; it refreshes once as soon as you're back. While you work, everything stays as up to date as before.

## Fixed

- The running time no longer rolls its seconds: that animation kept tikjot at about 40 % CPU with the popover or main window open, and could freeze the menu bar when working on two monitors. Now it's about 1 %.
- The popover closes when the menu bar moves to another monitor, instead of getting stuck between screens.
