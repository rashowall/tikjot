Open a task in Jira straight from tikjot, a calmer Day inspector, and older worklogs that no longer get stuck as Update or Failed.

## Improved

- Open a task in Jira from where you see it: "Open in Jira ↗" next to the task in the Day inspector, "Open … in Jira" when you right-click a block in Day or an entry in the popover, and a link on the task key when you hover a row in Review & send. In the popover and the Day inspector, hold ⌘ over the task and click — a plain click still picks another task. The link always goes to the task's own Jira site.
- Right-click menus in Day and in the popover have icons, groups and the shortcuts you can use.
- The Day inspector is calmer: instead of a list of shortcuts at the bottom there is one line, "? All keyboard shortcuts", which opens the full list (so does ? or Help › Keyboard Shortcuts). The buttons' tooltips name their shortcut.

## Fixed

- Older worklogs with several descriptions could show "Update" — and on a closed task "Failed" — although Jira already had them right. They show as sent now; nothing is sent again. Thanks for the report!
- When Jira refuses to change a worklog that is already there (for example the task is closed), the row now offers Take Jira's instead of Pick another task: the worklog stays as it is in Jira and tikjot follows it.
- Take Jira's kept a comment of several points as one line, so the worklog kept looking changed. Each point is now a line of the description.
