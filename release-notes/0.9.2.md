Smoother tracking from the menu bar, screens that match the design everywhere, confetti when your day is in Jira, and a clearer install window.

## New

- When a whole day or week lands in Jira, a short burst of confetti says so.
- The download opens a clear install window: drag tikjot to Applications, then open it from there.
- Settings › General has a Check for updates button next to the version.

## Improved

- Start (▶) and ⌥⌘T put you straight into "What are you working on?" with your recent descriptions; the task list opens with Tab.
- Editing a description (in the menu bar or the Day window) offers the same earlier entries as starting one; pick one to take its description and task.
- Projects: new project, merging, a key that belongs to another project and archived projects look as designed and explain what happens before you confirm.
- Statistics: the period (presets and a two-month calendar), project and billable filters look as designed.
- Many smaller screens look as designed: keyboard shortcuts (?), command bar (⌘K), add absence, several selected entries, picking a task, free-time suggestions, the date picker, Unsent selection, adding a task row in the week, company days off, the backup password and an empty or vacation day.
- Settings › General is shorter (8 rows instead of 12).
- Every dialog and popover uses the app's own surface, so nothing gets lost in dark mode.

## Fixed

- The menu bar shows tikjot's menus when its window is in front (it used to keep the previous app's).
- A start, end or length you typed is saved when you click Done, even without Return. The menu-bar entry editor no longer has "Open in Day".
- Typing a time into a week cell works as you type it ("1h30" no longer splits), ↩ sets it, and clicking elsewhere keeps it.
- Clicking outside a text field in the main window ends the editing.
- Add absence: a date range shows both dates, and the labels no longer shrink for Doctor.
- In the Day view, the sync icons line up on short entries too.
