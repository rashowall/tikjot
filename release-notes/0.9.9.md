Fixes from the latest round of beta feedback: the popover list no longer hides under the capsule, Statistics fit a whole quarter, and imported worklogs that run past midnight stay as they are in Jira.

## Improved

- Pinned tasks now win a tie while you type in the task picker. A closer match still comes first.
- Until you pin your first task, the picker shows a short hint: "Pin a task to keep it on top · ⌘P".
- The star in the task picker stays at the right edge and no longer jumps when you highlight a row.
- Click anywhere in a field to type in it, including on its unit ("days") or the empty space. Before, a click had to land on the number itself.
- The popover always opens at today, at the top of the list.

## Fixed

- The popover's list could slip under the "What are you working on?" capsule, hiding today's entries until you restarted tikjot. Thanks for the report!
- Statistics: with a long period, like a quarter, the trend chart squeezed "By project" into a thin column. The bars now fit the card.
- An imported Jira worklog that ran past midnight was split in two: its first day looked shorter than in Jira, and an edit sent it back as two worklogs. It now stays whole on the day it started, like in Jira.
