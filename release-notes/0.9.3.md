Smarter task search, a clearer way to start a timer, and a simpler first run — the first round of beta feedback.

## New

- Picking a task feels like mind reading: tasks you logged come first (also closed ones), then tasks assigned to you, and other people's tasks only below under "More in Jira". Typing a project key such as "WEB" brings your own WEB tasks to the top.
- ↩ on a new description takes you straight to choosing its task, with a clear "Step 2 · Pick a task for …" header. The tag button next to Start does the same. ⌘↩ starts without a task.
- After the first run, tikjot shows its two places once: the menu bar to track, the window to fix your day and send it to Jira.
- Connecting another Jira imports its history right in the dialog and lets you pick its projects there.
- The free-time popover in the Day window offers "Doctor".

## Improved

- The menu bar footer: settings (gear) and Open tikjot on the left, Review & send alone on the right.
- The menu bar shows more of your day — it grows up to ¾ of the screen.
- Resizing short entries in the Day window is steady: handles sit outside small blocks, the edge snaps only after a real move, and a pill shows the new time.
- First run: no "balance carried over" question (it stays in Settings › Work time), Back where you'd expect it, and projects you can untick or rename in place.
- Absence types say plainly "in days" or "in hours".
- Entry detail: Continue, Merge, Split and Delete in a tidy 2×2.

## Fixed

- Starting without a task no longer quietly puts the entry into a guessed project.
- Search no longer matches scattered letters inside unrelated words.
- Renaming a project saves when you click elsewhere, not only with Return.
- The Jira tab in Settings shows its icon on macOS 14.
- Projects found in a newly connected Jira are kept after a restart.
