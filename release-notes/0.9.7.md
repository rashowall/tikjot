A smarter "You were away" card after you switch tasks, and Connect Jira that finds out by itself whether it's Cloud or Data Center.

## Improved

- Connect Jira checks the address you type and sets Jira Cloud or Data Center / Server for you, with the right fields. If the address can't be reached (VPN off), you choose as before. Connecting with the wrong type now says so and switches.
- The "You were away" card has an × (keeps the time as it is).

## Fixed

- If you switched to another task without answering the "You were away" card, its buttons acted on the new timer: "Stop at…" could delete it. Now the card names the task you were away from, and "Remove 45m" takes the time out of that entry only. The card goes away by itself once there's nothing left to answer.
