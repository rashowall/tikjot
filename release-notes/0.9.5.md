A Day overview with everything you did, a calmer tikjot when your VPN is off, and a better look at the rare menu bar hiccups.

## New

- Day overview: in the Day window, next to Add absence…, one sheet with the day's totals, how it split between tasks, and every task with all its descriptions and times — handy for a standup or a report.
- Check for Updates… in the tikjot menu and in the menu bar icon's right-click menu.

## Improved

- The Day panel is simpler: the progress bar and your tasks, without the summary box.
- With the VPN off (or Jira asking you to sign in), tikjot stays quiet: it tries the unreachable connection again less and less often, and the others keep working. When the network changes it checks again within 10 seconds.
- In the popover's entry editor, ↩ in a time field applies the time; a second ↩ is Done.
- Menu items have icons on macOS 26.

## Fixed

- The Jira banner no longer blinks when one of two connections is reachable and the other isn't.
- A guard against the popover resizing itself over and over (seen as high CPU and a blinking popover).
- When tikjot stops responding for a moment or uses a lot of CPU, its log now says what it was doing, so the cause can be found (Settings › Export diagnostics…).
