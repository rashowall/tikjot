Hour absences that step by the hour, a task search that tells you when Jira isn't answering — the second round of beta feedback.

## New

- Doctor and other hour absences: one click for 1h, 2h, 4h or Rest of the day (from the start to the end of your working day).
- A future day in the Day window has its own picture: the page circling a day ahead, next to Add absence…

## Improved

- In hour absences ↑↓ moves the start and the length by an hour (⇧ by 15 minutes), and the length never drops under 1 hour.
- Opening a task picker quietly fetches tasks from Jira when the last update is more than 5 minutes old, so a task created a moment ago is there without typing.
- When the Jira search gets no answer (VPN off, company sign-in), the picker says "Jira isn't answering · saved tasks only" instead of just missing tasks.

## Fixed

- In the entry detail, the project line under the task no longer shows through the open task picker.
- Time and length fields show the new value right away when you press ↑↓ (they were one step behind).
- tikjot now notes in its log when it stops responding for a moment, so a rare menu bar hiccup can be traced (Settings › Export diagnostics…).
