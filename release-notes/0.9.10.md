Fixes from this week's beta feedback: a sent day with descriptions of several lines no longer looks unsent, typing in the task search keeps up again, and the popover behaves better with two displays.

## Improved

- Descriptions with several lines: lists show the first line and a small "+2" for the rest (hover shows them all), and the Day timeline shows as many lines as the block has room for. In Jira every line becomes one point of the worklog's list — the same on Data Center and Cloud.
- Review & send shows the comment the way Jira will: one point per line.
- Merge joins only neighbouring entries of the same task. When it can't, it says why: another entry in between, a running timer, or no earlier entry of the task.
- When a merge spans an empty gap, the message says how much time the gap adds ("Merged · +25m from the gap"), with Undo.

## Fixed

- A sent day with descriptions of several lines kept showing "Update", and sending it again changed nothing. It now shows as sent — your worklogs in Jira were fine all along. Thanks for the report!
- Typing in the task search could lag by seconds. The search now does a lot less work while you type, which should fix it — if it still lags, please send your diagnostics (Settings › General › Export diagnostics…).
- With two displays the popover could blink in the menu bar and leave a spinning cursor. It no longer follows the icon to the other display and closes at once when the menu bar moves, which should help — we couldn't test it on two displays yet. If you still see it, please send your diagnostics; they now list your displays.
- Merging two entries of the same task with another task between them laid the merged entry over it.
