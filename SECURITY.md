# Security

tikjot keeps your time data encrypted on your Mac and talks only to your own Jira. If you find a way around that —
for example a way to read the data or a Jira token, to send data anywhere else, or to make tikjot write to Jira
without you pressing Send — please tell us privately:

- **GitHub:** Security › "Report a vulnerability" (private advisory), or
- **E-mail:** hello (at) tikjot.com with "security" in the subject.

Please don't open a public issue for security problems. You'll get an answer within a week; tikjot is maintained
by one person in their free time, so fixes may take a little longer — you'll be kept in the loop and credited if
you like.

Supported versions: the latest release.
